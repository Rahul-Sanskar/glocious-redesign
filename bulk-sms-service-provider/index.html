Bulk SMS Best Practices for Delivery, Clarity and Response

Write clear SMS messages, segment the right audience, optimise timing and track performance so your Bulk SMS campaigns stay useful, compliant and measurable.

Keep Messages Concise & Clear Keep one goal per SMS. Use simple language, a clear CTA, and a template-aligned format. Avoid stuffing multiple links or messages into one send.
Use Personalisation & Segmentation Segment by behavior (new vs returning, city, product interest). Use light personalization like {name} or {order_id} without adding sensitive details.
A/B Test Message Variants Test one change at a time (offer vs CTA vs tone). Compare delivery + clicks/responses to learn what actually improves outcomes.
Optimise Send Times Using AI Schedule promotional campaigns for high-attention windows; keep OTP/alerts instant. Use reports to find times that reduce pending and improve response.
Clean Contact Lists Regularly Remove duplicates and invalid numbers regularly. Respect opt-outs and keep lists clean to protect deliverability and reporting accuracy.
Always Include Opt-Out Instructions For promotional traffic, include an opt-out line where applicable and ensure your opt-out flow works reliably end-to-end.
Use Multi-Channel Fallback Intelligently If SMS is delayed or restricted, trigger WhatsApp/RCS/Voice fallback based on consent and channel availability—so critical messages still reach users.
