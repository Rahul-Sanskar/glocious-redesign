What is RCS?

The Next Generation of Business Messaging

RCS (Rich Communication Services) is the modern upgrade to SMS, designed to bring app-like experiences directly into the native Android messaging app — with no additional app required by the customer.

With RCS Business Messaging, you can send verified, branded messages that include images, videos, carousels, quick-reply buttons, and CTAs — all while maintaining the simplicity and reach of SMS.

Glocious Infotech handles end-to-end RCS onboarding: brand registration, Google verification, template creation, API integration, and campaign management.
